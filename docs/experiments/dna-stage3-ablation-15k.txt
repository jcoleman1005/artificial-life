Ablation assay, DNA Stage 3 (growable brain). Seeds 1-8; each evolved 15000 ticks with dnaEnabled, dnaVariationEnabled, dnaGrowableBrain on.
Ablation: genome -> stripAcquiredTopology(genome, 'effective') (genes of choice-variant acquired units only; choice-invariant acquired units kept); dense brain and other traits unchanged; children inherit the stripped genome.
survivalEarly = share of original half members alive after 100 ticks; survival = at the end of the window.
Cohort: living organisms with choice-variant (effective) acquired units > 0. B1: 10 replicates × 500 ticks, cohort split in two equal random halves, one ablated; sham = same split and stream, nobody ablated.
B2: up to 40 cohort organisms per seed alone in 4 fixed 24×24 arenas × 200 ticks, mutation off, intact vs ablated.
Caveat: ablated organisms save dnaUpkeepPerConnection on the acquired connections removed and dnaUpkeepPerGene on removed genes (a small energy advantage).
