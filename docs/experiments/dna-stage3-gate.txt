DNA Stage 3 (growable brain) gate, rerun — 2026-10-27
=====================================================

Gate criterion: removing acquired connections must measurably degrade fitness or survival in at least a subset of seeds.

VERDICT: MET, in one seed of eight (seed 6), with the reverse effect in two others (seeds 4 and 8) and nothing pooled.
- Seed 6: stripping the choice-variant acquired units (level 'effective', the gate default) costs the ablated half heavily in every
  assay: in situ births −52.7 vs the sham (10/10 replicates, p = 0.002, the floor for 10 replicates), early survival −0.225 (10/10,
  p = 0.002), mean energy −2.79 (10/10, p = 0.002); in the standardized arenas, 66.7 vs 29.0 births (16 organisms better intact,
  2 better ablated, p < 0.001) and founders live 40% less of the window (p < 0.001). The 'units' control (every gene on units ≥ 6)
  gives the same numbers. The in situ p of 0.002 alone would not survive a Bonferroni correction over 8 seeds × 5 measures (0.08),
  but the arena assay is independent, its p < 0.001 survives ×8, and three in situ measures agree in direction in all 10 replicates.
- Since the rerun's two changes, flipped keys cannot reach founder connections and the ablation only removes acquired units that can
  change a choice, so this degrade is due to acquired topology proper, unlike the first gate's seed 2 result (wrapped keys acting as
  founder weight changes).
- Seeds 4 and 8 go the other way: ablated organisms do BETTER. Seed 4: in situ births +8.1 for the ablated half (9/10, p = 0.037)
  and arena births 3.8 vs 1.4 (9 vs 1 organisms, p = 0.003). Seed 8: in situ births +20.5 for the ablated half (9/10, p = 0.004), arena null.
  Seeds 1, 2, 3, 5, 7: nothing distinguishable from the sham (one isolated p = 0.043, seed 3 early survival).
- Pooled over seeds every in situ measure is null (births 2/8 seeds > 0, sign-flip p = 1.00; early survival p = 0.39; energy 7/8,
  p = 0.21). The pooled arena difference (+4.6 births, p = 0.019) is carried by seed 6 (76 vs 69 organisms better intact).
- Reading: acquired units now evolve everywhere (100% of organisms, ~4.3 units each at 15,000 ticks) and matter strongly in one
  lineage; across seeds their effect is idiosyncratic in sign, so "acquired topology is generally adaptive" is NOT supported.

What changed since the first gate (commit 64a735b, both user-approved)
1. Addressing: a brain gene key whose unit field is ≥ 6 maps to 6 + (u − 6) mod (M − 6), a new unit; skip/input indices ≥ 52 and
   output-bias indices ≠ 0 are silent. A flipped key can no longer land on a founder connection.
2. Ablation default level 'effective': strips only the genes of acquired units with a live in-edge from an input that differs between
   actions (circuit.effectiveAcquiredUnits). Choice-invariant units (constant, or reading only bias/energy/carried/crowding/register0–2)
   stay. Cohort = organisms with effectiveAcquiredUnits > 0.
3. Horizon 15,000 ticks (was 5,000), as the user asked.

Caveats
- Upkeep: ablated organisms stop paying dnaUpkeepPerConnection (0.00005) on removed acquired connections and dnaUpkeepPerGene (0.00005)
  on removed genes. 'effective': at most ~0.016 energy/tick (seeds 2, 3: ~200 genes + ~120 connections), ~0.006 in seed 6, ~0.0014 in seed 8.
  'units': up to ~0.023/tick. This favours the ablated half, so it works against the seed 6 degrade and could contribute to the seed 4
  advantage (~0.008/tick); it cannot explain seed 8 (~0.0014/tick but −20.5 births).
- Unadjusted p-values: 8 seeds × 5 measures × 2 levels in situ plus the arenas. 0.002 is the smallest p possible with 10 replicates;
  seed 6 rests on its consistency (10/10 in three measures) and on the independent arena assay, not on one p-value.
- Rounding near-ties: action scores are rounded to 3 decimals, so a choice-invariant unit can still change a choice at a near-tie.
  Only seed 8 has choice-invariant units (~0.31 per organism), which 'effective' leaves and 'units' removes.
- Mating between halves: births/descendants through a mating between the two halves count for both halves. Matings are ~2% of births,
  so the bias toward zero is small.
- Children of ablated organisms inherit the stripped genome: shorter genomes mean fewer Stage 2 mutations per birth and lower upkeep for
  the lineage (affects descendants, not member energy or survival). Removing genes also shifts the world's random stream relative to the sham.
- End-of-window survival is uninformative (0 in 6 seeds); survival after 100 ticks is the survival measure.
- Extinctions: Part A growable off died out in seeds 1, 5, 6 (seed 6 almost at once: avgPop 4, 583 births in all), growable on in none.
  Seed 6, the one seed where acquired units clearly matter, is also the seed where growable off fails at once; this is one coincidence,
  not evidence of a link.
- Single evolved world per seed and one 15,000-tick horizon; the effect belongs to the lineage that evolved there.
- Perf rows are reused from the first gate (not rerun).
- No parameters were changed. No script bug was found or fixed.
