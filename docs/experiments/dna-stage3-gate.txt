DNA Stage 3 (growable brain) gate — 2026-10-27
================================================

Gate criterion: removing acquired connections must measurably degrade fitness or survival in at least a subset of seeds.

VERDICT: INCONCLUSIVE (leaning null for acquired topology proper).
- Under the ablation as specified (strip 'all': every brain gene the growable-off decode ignores), the criterion is met in 1 of 6 testable seeds:
  seed 2 shows a clear loss in both assays (in situ births −17 per half vs sham, 10/10 replicates, p = 0.002, the floor for 10 replicates;
  standardized arenas 32.1 vs 10.5 births, 16 vs 3 organisms, p < 0.001). Seed 1 is suggestive only (descendants alive +29 vs sham, 9/10, p = 0.033;
  arenas 28.3 vs 19.1 births, p = 0.12). Seeds 3, 4, 6, 7: no effect distinguishable from the sham.
- But the 'all' strip removes far more than the acquired hidden units: 34–104 genes per cohort member, of which only 16–44 sit on units ≥ 6
  (the rest are wrapped keys, which the growable brain folds onto the founders' existing slots, i.e. weight changes, not new topology).
  The control ablation that removes ONLY genes on units ≥ 6 ('units', i.e. exactly the acquired units/connections) shows no reliable degrade:
  seed 2 in situ births +6.8 vs sham (7/10, p = 0.037, not significant once the ~30 per-seed tests are counted), and in the arenas seed 2
  goes the other way (ablated 35.7 vs intact 32.1 births, p = 0.018). Pooled across seeds every measure is null (sign-flip p ≥ 0.4).
- So: growable-only gene expression as a whole matters in one seed, and what matters there is the re-expressed wrapped keys, not the
  acquired hidden units. For acquired connections in the strict sense (circuit.acquiredConnections), the result is null at this sample size.
  A null here is weak evidence of absence: cohorts are small (1–1.5 acquired units, 2.5–5 acquired connections per member), and only
  ~30% of organisms carry any.

Caveats
- Seed 8 goes extinct with growable on (seed 6 with it off); seed 5 has a single organism with acquired topology (untestable). 6 seeds tested.
- Ablated organisms stop paying dnaUpkeepPerConnection (0.00005 per acquired connection) and dnaUpkeepPerGene (0.00005 per removed gene):
  at most ~0.005 energy/tick for 'all' in seed 1, ~0.002 for 'units' — small, but it biases toward the ablated half.
- Window 500 ticks is longer than most lifespans: end-of-window survival of the original members is ~0 in 5 of 6 seeds (uninformative);
  survival after 100 ticks (survivalEarly) is reported instead and shows no degrade (seed 1, 'all': ablated survive slightly MORE, p = 0.031,
  consistent with them reproducing less and saving upkeep).
- "Live" acquired units (with at least one input) are 90–100% of acquired units, but a unit whose inputs do not vary across a tick's
  candidate actions adds the same offset to every action and cannot change an argmax choice; the assay does not separate those.
- In the B1 design births/descendants through a mating between the two halves count for both halves.
- 10 replicates per seed; sign-flip p-values are unadjusted. Evolution 5000 ticks only.
- No parameters were changed to make the gate pass.
