# Growth and ageing (world change, roadmap item 11)

Status: spec, 2026-10-28. A world change: follow `WORLD_RULES.md` strictly (switches, switch-off identity, snapshots, test budget). Needs the user's agreement as a decision (record in `docs/decisions.md` at merge; the coordinator adds the AGENTS.md index line).

## Goal
Organisms hatch small, grow to their adult size over time, can only breed once mature, and decline with age. Gives juvenile vulnerability, a cost to being born early, generation turnover, and a reason for parental care. Nothing behavioural is hardcoded: only bodies and costs change.

## Mechanics (two switches)
`growthEnabled` (default on) and `ageingEnabled` (default on), independent, each the lock for its control arm. Both off = bit-identical to today (fingerprint in `docs/STATUS.md` is the baseline).

**Growth.** Add `maturity` in [0, 1] to Organism (state, snapshotted). Newborns/hatchlings start at `hatchMaturity` (0.35). `growthTicks` (600) to reach 1 if fed. Each tick growth adds `1/growthTicks` only while `energy ≥ growthEnergyFloor × maxEnergy` (growth stalls when starving, so it is food-limited), and costs `growthCostPerTick` extra energy (heat, counted in an energy ledger so conservation tests still hold). `bodyScale = hatchScale + (1 - hatchScale) × maturity` multiplies the *effective* size from the existing size gene: `maxEnergy`, basal metabolism (Kleiber exponent, already in code), movement cost, bite size/plant yield and carry capacity, visual radius (renderer only). Adult-size genes remain inherited; juveniles are weaker (smaller bite, smaller reserves) but cheaper to run.
- **Maturity gate:** REPRODUCE and MATE are illegal below `matureAt` (0.9). Illegal actions are masked like other hard constraints (`policy.ts`), not penalised.
- Interaction with `childhoodTicks` (parent-sensing window): leave it unchanged; document how growth overlaps it. Clone/mating/egg energy accounting stays as is; a hatchling's initial energy is clamped to its small `maxEnergy`, and any excess is dissipated and counted in the existing loss ledgers.
- Brain input: append-only `maturity` (0..1) to `BRAIN_INPUTS` with an `upgradeBrain` entry, zero head start and zero weight for founders; update `BASE_BRAIN_INPUT_COUNT` bookkeeping per existing convention and the `tests/defaults.test.ts` pins if needed. Report the append.

**Ageing.** After `senescenceAge` (4000 ticks) metabolism rises by `senescenceRate` per tick of extra age (multiplier `1 + senescenceRate × (age - senescenceAge)`), and above `maxAge` (8000) the organism dies of old age (death cause "old age", leaves carrion as normal). No random hazard draws needed (deterministic by age). Optional second step, only if cheap: an inherited `senescenceAge` gene with a trade-off (longer life costs upkeep) — propose in the report, don't build without agreement.

## Surfaces
- Inspector / organism card: maturity bar and "juvenile/adult/senescent" badge; age vs lifespan.
- Renderer: body radius uses `bodyScale`; render-only.
- Metrics: mean maturity, juvenile fraction, deaths by old age, mean lifespan (existing field), included in CSV and experiment output.
- Events feed: no per-individual events (world events only); optionally a population-level "old age deaths spike" if trivial.
- Settings: toggles for both under Genetics/Life history.

## Process (per WORLD_RULES.md)
1. Record `npm run fingerprint` before; after, `--set growthEnabled=false --set ageingEnabled=false` must match exactly. Extend `scripts/fingerprint.ts` to hash maturity when on. Record new default fingerprints in the agent's report (the coordinator writes STATUS.md).
2. Snapshots: `maturity` goes into `exportState()`/`fromState()`; optional field defaulting to 1 for old saves (so old worlds load as adults). Bump version if the repo convention requires it.
3. Test budget only: smoke run, round trip, one hand-built mechanism test (growth stall when starving; REPRODUCE masked when immature; energy conservation incl. growth heat; old-age death at `maxAge`), seeded repeatability, perf at 500 organisms (<5%).
4. Do NOT run the full 8-seed gate; run a quick look (2 seeds × 3000 ticks, growth on vs off) and report population curves. The default-on decision for ageing/growth is the user's after seeing it; if the quick look shows collapse, ship that switch default **off** and say so.
5. Log every tuned constant in the Parameter log of `docs/findings.md` and pin head starts in `tests/defaults.test.ts`.

## As built (2026-10-28, re-implemented on the current branch)

Port of the first build (old base, `0b633a3`) onto the branch that has since gained weather, the nutrient cycle, Red Queen, lineage, the brain viewer, species detection and the new organism card. Plus a new inherited gene, `growthDuration`. Switches `growthEnabled`, `ageingEnabled`, `growthGeneEnabled`, all on by default (the ship-on decision is the user's: see Quick look).

**Code.** `src/sim/growth.ts` (helpers: `maturityOf`, `bodyScaleOf`, `isMature`, `carryCapacityOf`, `senescenceFactorOf`, `diesOfOldAge`, `lifeStageOf`, and the gene helpers `growthDurationOf`, `growthRateOf`, `growthCostOf`); small hunks in `traits.ts`, `lifehistory.ts`, `sensors.ts`, `policy.ts`, `brain.ts`, `dna.ts`, `world.ts`, `protocol.ts`, `history.ts`, `metrics.ts`, `renderer.ts`, `inspector.ts`, `organism-card.ts`, `metrics-view.ts`, `history-view.ts`; `scripts/fingerprint.ts`, `scripts/experiment.ts`; `tests/growth.test.ts` and pins in `tests/defaults.test.ts`; constants in `docs/findings.md` (Parameter log).

**State.** `Organism.maturity?` (0..1), set only while growth is on: founders and immigrants 1, newborns and hatchlings `hatchMaturity`. Absent means grown. `Organism.growthDuration?` (the gene). `WorldState.version` stays 1: counters `growthHeatTotal`, `growthEnergyLostTotal`, `deathsOldAgeTotal` load as 0, and a save whose config lacks `growthEnabled` / `ageingEnabled` loads with both **off** (so an old world continues bit-identically). A save made with growth on but without the gene loads with every organism falling back to `growthTicks` (`growthDurationOf`), and its descendants get the founder value (`lifeForConfig`).

**Config.** `growthEnabled` true, `hatchMaturity` 0.35, `hatchScale` 0.4, `growthTicks` 100, `growthEnergyFloor` 0.4, `growthCostPerTick` 0.05, `matureAt` 0.9; `growthGeneEnabled` true, `growthDurationFounder` 100, `growthDurationMin` 20, `growthDurationMax` 800, `growthDurationStep` 10, `growthDurationCostExponent` 1.5; `ageingEnabled` true, `senescenceAge` 4000, `senescenceRate` 0.001, `maxAge` 8000.

**Growth.** `bodyScale = hatchScale + (1 - hatchScale) x maturity` (a newborn is 0.61). It multiplies the maximum energy (reserves; every cap and ratio built on it), the Kleiber base upkeep and movement cost (effective size = size gene x bodyScale), plant yield, carrion yield and bite, and carry capacity (rounded, at least 1 for a carrier; upkeep still charged on the inherited capacity). Each tick, after the action, a juvenile whose energy is at least `growthEnergyFloor x maxEnergyOf` gains `1 / duration` maturity and pays heat (in `growthHeatTotal` and in the decision record's `energyDelta`); below the floor it stalls. REPRODUCE and MATE are masked below `matureAt` (`sensors.ts` canReproduce, `policy.ts` readyToBreed, and again in `World.mate`). A newborn's energy is clamped to its small body's capacity; the excess is lost and counted in `growthEnergyLostTotal`. `childhoodTicks` is unchanged.

**Growth-duration gene (trait 55, `growthDuration`).** A life-history gene in the `LIFE_GENES` table, so it is inherited by clones (`inheritLife`), by legacy mating (`recombine`) and by DNA mating (`crossGenomes` by key), mutates one step (`growthDurationStep`) with probability `traitMutationRate`, and is clamped to [`growthDurationMin`, `growthDurationMax`] exactly like clutch and detox. It is carried only while `growthEnabled && growthGeneEnabled`; otherwise `growthDurationOf` returns the fixed `growthTicks` and nothing else changes (switch-off test: an organism with a stray `growthDuration` still grows at `1/growthTicks` and pays `growthCostPerTick`). The trade-off is in the heat only: heat per growing tick is `growthCostPerTick x (growthTicks / duration)^growthDurationCostExponent`; growing from 0 to 1 takes `duration` such ticks, so the total heat is `growthCostPerTick x growthTicks^e x duration^(1 - e)`. With the default e = 1.5 that is proportional to duration^-0.5: a 4x faster grower pays 2x the heat, a 4x slower one pays half. (e = 1 would make the total independent of duration; e < 1 would reward fast growth outright, so the config knob is the whole trade-off and no rule names a preferred duration.) The cost of being slow is not a penalty added by a rule but what a small body already is: a juvenile has 0.4-1.0 of adult reserves and bite, and cannot breed until `matureAt`, so a slow grower spends longer unable to breed and to compete for food. Nothing is tuned to make any duration win.

**Ageing.** `metabolismCost` is multiplied by `1 + senescenceRate x (age - senescenceAge)` past `senescenceAge`. At `age >= maxAge` the organism dies in Phase 5 (`deathsOldAgeTotal`), leaving carrion from `carrionFromBody(energy left)`. No random draws. The optional `senescenceAge` gene is not built.

**Brain input (append-only), and why the width no longer follows the switch.** `maturity` (0..1; 1 for a grown body or with growth off) is appended last in `BRAIN_INPUTS`: index **56**, count now 57 (after the weather inputs 52-54 and `lastBiteYield` 55). The first build made brain width follow `growthEnabled` because that was the only way to keep its switch off bit-identical. The current branch already solved the same problem for weather and plant defense differently, and that pattern is reused: brains always have 57 inputs, and an input whose feature switch is off gets zero weights that are never drawn, mutated or encoded as genes (`brainInputActive(i, config)` in `brain.ts`: `createHeadStartBrain` draws no `small()` for it, `mutateBrain` freezes it, `encodeOrganismDNA` omits its genes, `slotKeys` has no insertion slots for it, `upgradeGenome` does not add its gene, `isExpressed` treats it as silent). Zero weights add exactly 0 to every score, and no PRNG draw differs, so the world with both switches off is bit-identical. This avoids variable-width brains (and the mixed-width cases `crossBrains`, `brainDrift` and the brain viewer would otherwise need). Head start 0, hidden weights small random draws while growth is on, so whether brains use maturity is left to evolve. A save from before the input upgrades through `upgradeBrain` (zero hidden weights, head start 0).

**Test budget.** `tests/growth.test.ts`: growth stalls when starving and advances and pays heat when fed; REPRODUCE/MATE masked below `matureAt`; body scaling; newborn maturity and capacity; old-age death at `maxAge` with carrion and upkeep rise; the brain input appended at 56, its switch-off draw count equal to the pre-growth count, and old narrow brains upgraded; the gene: range and presence, fallback to `growthTicks`, total-heat closed form and monotonicity, faster grower matures sooner and pays more heat (and slower the reverse) in a hand-built isolated run, gene-off identity, clone and mating inheritance (legacy and DNA), clamping, genome round trip and missing-gene decode, snapshot with and without the gene; 2 x 400-tick smoke; same-seed repeatability; snapshot round trip with the switches on and off; pre-growth save loads with both off. `tests/sex.test.ts`: three energy-accounting tests set `growthEnabled: false` (stated in each) so a newborn's small body does not clip the shares they check.

**Open points.** (1) The default-on decision and `growthTicks` (100; the full gate was not run). (2) The gene did not move in the quick look; whether it moves over longer runs, and with which step, is untested. (3) Ageing at 4000/8000 is inert here (mean lifespans 65-250 ticks); decide whether to rescale after an age histogram from a long run. (4) The optional inherited `senescenceAge` gene is proposed, not built. (5) Juvenile bites still use up a whole food unit for 0.61 of the energy. (6) A live toggle of `growthEnabled` in a running world leaves existing organisms without the gene fields (they fall back to `growthTicks`).
